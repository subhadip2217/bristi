The name

In Bangla, rain is not weather.

বৃষ্টি arrives without asking. It changes the colour of the afternoon, then leaves the world smelling like it remembers you.

Some names are labels. Hers is a climate. Soft on the glass, loud on the tin roof, and somehow both at once — which is a rare talent in a person.

This page is just that idea, stretched out: a small monsoon made of type, a photograph, and a few things you can actually play with.
